# RFM-Customer-Segmentation-Analysis
# 📊 E-Commerce Customer Segmentation using RFM Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project performing **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** customer segmentation on transactional data (`online_retail.csv`) to identify key customer cohorts and evaluate their revenue contribution.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
* **Objective:** Clean raw transactional records, score customer purchasing patterns across Recency, Frequency, and Monetary dimensions, and group customers into actionable business segments.
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook.

---

## ⚙️ Data Cleaning & Processing Pipeline
1. **Handling Missing Values:** Filtered out records without `CustomerID` (~135k rows).
2. **Transaction Filtering:** Excluded cancelled invoices (starting with `'C'`), zero/negative quantities, and unit prices.
3. **Stock Filtering:** Removed non-product transactions (`POST`, `D`, `M`, `BANK CHARGES`, etc.).
4. **RFM Scoring:**
   * **Recency ($R$):** Days since the customer's last purchase relative to the reference date.
   * **Frequency ($F$):** Number of unique purchase invoices per customer.
   * **Monetary ($M$):** Total revenue generated by the customer.
   * Scored from $1$ to $5$ using quintile binning (`pd.qcut` with rank handling).

---

## 🎯 Customer Segments Defined
* **Champions:** High recency, high frequency, and high monetary value ($R \ge 4, F \ge 4$).
* **Potential Loyalists:** Recent buyers with moderate frequency ($R \ge 3, F \in [2, 3]$).
* **New Customers:** High recency but single-time buyers ($R \ge 4, F = 1$).
* **At Risk (Can't Lose Them):** Previously frequent shoppers who haven't returned recently ($R \le 2, F \ge 4$).
* **Lost:** Low recency and single purchase ($R = 1, F = 1$).
* **Others:** General customer base with mixed scores.

### Key Business Takeaways:
* **80/20 Rule in Action:** **Champions** represent ~26% of the customer base but drive **~67% of total revenue**.
* **Churn Prevention Priority:** **At Risk** segment accounts for ~5% of revenue; targeted win-back campaigns and incentives are critical before complete churn.
* **Growth Opportunity:** **Potential Loyalists** (~20% volume) can be nurtured into Champions through loyalty programs and personalized upselling.
